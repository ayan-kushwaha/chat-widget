Solution: Hybrid System — 2 Zones

Zone 1 — LIQUID MORPH (abhi hua)
  idle ↔ sad ↔ happy ↔ thinking ↔ angry ↔ sleepy

  love (hearts) | star (diamonds) | dizzy (spirals)

Ye nano-difference hai blink-swap se:

Fade swap: shape dissolve hoti hai → nayi shape dissolve in hoti hai (opacity 0→1) — NATURAL LAGTA HAI
