📘 Project: Cluaiz AI Bot (The Business Companion)
1. Vision & Concept
The Goal: Ek desktop-sized, cute, mobile robot jo businessman ka "Physical Assistant" hoga.

Role-Switching: Ye sirf ek robot nahi, balki 12 alag "AI Specialists" (jaise Shreya for Sales, Ali for Tech) ka avatar hai.

Target: Indian SMBs aur Freelancers jo ek mehangi team afford nahi kar sakte, wo ₹30,000 - ₹40,000 mein ek "AI Workforce" ghar le jayenge.

2. Hardware Architecture (The Body)
The Brain (NVIDIA Jetson Nano): 128-core GPU power jo complex AI models aur real-time face tracking ko makkhan ki tarah chalayega.

Senses (Human-Like):

Vision: Wide-angle camera with AI vision (YOLO/MediaPipe) logon ko pehchanne ke liye.

Hearing: 4-6 Mic array jo aawaz ki direction pehchan sake.

Touch: Capacitive sensors sir aur body par "Petting" feel ke liye.

Balance: 6-axis Gyroscope dashboard par ya window par baithne ke liye.

Display (Eyes): 2 OLED screens jo Emo-like 3D expressions aur 3D Particle Matrix dikhayengi.

3. Software & AI Stack (The Soul)
Local LLM (BitNet): Hum BitNet b1.58 ya quantized Llama models use karenge jo Jetson Nano par locally (bina internet ke) baatein kar sakein.

The Display Matrix: Ek dynamic 3D Particle system jo AI ke emotions aur tasks ke hisaab se shape badlega (e.g., Radar for searching, Heart for happy).

Real-time Voice: Gemini Live API (Cloud) aur Faster-Whisper (Local) ka hybrid combination seamless conversation ke liye.

Memory: Qdrant Vector Database jo robot ko user ki har purani baat aur business data yaad dilayega.

4. Costing & Business Strategy
Prototype Cost: ₹25,000 - ₹30,000 per unit (Jetson Nano, sensors, 3D body).

Selling Price: ₹35,000 - ₹45,000 (Market price of Emo in India is high, but ours adds business value).

Marketing Hook: "Make in India" Business Assistant jo car dashboard se lekar office desk tak sath rahega.
