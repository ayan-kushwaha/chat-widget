## 🦾 Core Engine: The "BitMamba" Hybrid
At the heart of Cluaiz-AI lies the **BitMamba Engine**.
- **Mamba-3 SSM Core:** Eliminates $O(N^2)$ quadratic complexity, providing $O(N)$ linear scaling for infinite context horizons. [cite: 19, 113]
- **BitNet 1.58-bit Quantization:** Utilizes ternary $\{ -1, 0, 1 \}$ weights to achieve extreme VRAM compression, allowing a 2.8B parameter model to run with sub-1s TTFT on a 4GB system. [cite: 22, 38]

## 🧠 The 4-Pillar Nervous System
Inspired by standard industrial architecture, the engine is divided into four biological-inspired clusters:
1. **RUNTIME_SERVICES (Synapse-Router):** The high-speed decision intelligence that routes signals between memory, training, and execution. [cite: 27, 109]
2. **KERNEL_CORE (VRAM-Guardian):** A Rust-native reflex layer that maintains a strict **1GB OS / 3GB Engine** memory partition, ensuring system-wide stability. [cite: 31, 32]
3. **DATA_STACK (The 5-Pillar Hub):** A hybrid of 5 specialized databases (Neo4j, MongoDB, ClickHouse, Qdrant, SearxNG) for complex reasoning and episodic memory. [cite: 51, 122]
4. **PIPELINE_OPS (Evolution Suite):** A self-improving training loop that distillates local interactions into nightly LoRA updates. [cite: 102, 133]
